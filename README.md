# bakery-supply-chain-analysis
Supply chain analysis project focused on bakery inventory optimization, sales performance, and replenishment risk assessment using Excel.

# Bakery Inventory & Sales Optimization Analysis

## Business Problem
A bakery business must maintain sufficient inventory to meet customer demand while minimizing excess stock and inventory holding costs.

## Objectives
- Identify products with stockout risk
- Analyze inventory efficiency
- Evaluate sales performance
- Provide replenishment recommendations

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Table
- Data Visualization
- PowerPoint

## Key Findings
- Almond Cookies showed the highest stockout risk due to low inventory buffer and long supplier lead time
- Chocolate Cake demonstrated potential overstock exposure
- Butter Cookies recorded the strongest sales performance

## Recommendations
- Prioritize replenishment planning for Almond Cookies
- Reduce excess inventory exposure for Chocolate Cake
- Maintain inventory availability for high-demand products
